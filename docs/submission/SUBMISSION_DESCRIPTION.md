## Product Title
**JanSetu — AI for Digital Public Infrastructure and Governance**

## 2–3 Line Executive Summary
**JanSetu (जनसेतु)** is an AI-powered Digital Public Infrastructure platform enabling 1.4B Indian citizens to report civic grievances across 13 Indian languages via voice notes, WhatsApp, SMS, and IVR telephony. Powered by **Google Gemini**, JanSetu automatically transcribes native speech, extracts urgency, validates locations, and issues high-entropy tracking tokens for real-time progress monitoring. Integrated with a Zero-PII **Officials Console** and an authoritative **Public Funds** transparency dashboard, JanSetu bridges citizens and government with accountable, data-driven governance.

---

## Key Highlights
- **13 Indian Languages & Voice Telephony**: Full voice note, WhatsApp, SMS, and IVR accessibility for low-literacy and feature-phone users.
- **Resilient Google Gemini Pool**: 7-tier automatic model escalation ensures 100% availability on Google AI Studio free tier.
- **Zero-PII & Accountless Tracking**: HMAC-SHA256 privacy safeguards with non-sequential `JS-XXXX-XXXX` tokens.
- **Authoritative Public Funds Tracking**: Budget allocations, releases, and expenditures with strict stage precedence and anti-double-counting logic.
- **Production-Ready & Fully Tested**: 280 passing backend automated tests and optimized React 19 / Vite production builds.
